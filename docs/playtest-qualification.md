## Full-route campaign baseline

Campaigns now require one bounded 3D route from their fixed departure to the pursued player's position at query start. A safe, entity-ticking stock-band point on that witness becomes the spawn anchor. The server rechecks the witness at insertion; it does not load or generate chunks for route checks. Missing terrain or an exhausted budget does not authorize a spawn. The surface planner, persistent terrain atlas, immediate-anchor search, and their obsolete config knobs are removed. The campaign snapshot schema is unchanged; its retired fields cannot authorize an insertion. The existing live packet safety checks and post-spawn hunt/combat goals still apply.

For a disposable, explored test area, `/campaign force assault` or `/campaign force scout` queues the normal route without waiting for the dispatch timer. It does not instantly insert mobs, bypass regional familiarity, or fabricate a route through unexplored terrain. Use `/campaign status` or `/campaign inspect` to see progress and failures. These source notes do not qualify a packaged JAR or a full-pack session.

After removal of the retired planners, `./gradlew --no-daemon verifyFull` passed all 18 required Forge GameTests and the fast verification lane. The source fixtures cover rejection of a later packet after gate closure and reading a terrain edit from disk after its chunk unloads; neither is a full-pack or process-restart test.

## Primary shipped gameplay promise

The existing Forge mod runs a pack-owned, server-side overland campaign director: eligible living Survival players establish *regional presence* through actual online ticks, receive sparse scouts and warned, progress-gated pillager assaults, and face authored mixed rosters; group proximity, terrain-aware strategic travel, reachable local approaches, caps and campaign mob persistence govern delivery. An encounter is not a new craftable item or block. Source anchors: `PillagerCampaignsEvents.kt` (server ticks, online presence, terrain invalidation, command inspection), `PillagerCampaignsConfig.kt` (defaults/caps), `system/InvasionRoster.kt` (registered recruit types), `system/InvasionRuntime.kt` (mob materialization/cleanup), `data/PillagerWorldData.kt` (saved campaign state), `system/VirtualCampaignTerrain.kt` (live/saved terrain reads), and `invasion-core/src/main/...` (pure director, routing and presence). `gradle.properties` calls this version `0.5.4`; do not infer that the pack contains this exact source revision.

The player-facing acquisition path is **encounters through survival play**, not an item/recipe/GUI unlock: survival online presence and eligibility, open connected known terrain and elapsed eligible play lead to scout/assault scheduling (default scout 7,200–14,400 eligible ticks, assault 72,000–144,000; `PillagerCampaignsConfig.kt`). `system/InvasionRoster.kt` references vanilla pillagers/vindicators/witches/ravagers, optional Takes a Pillage and Savage & Ravage entity registry IDs. `system/CampaignTconLoadouts.kt` equips select campaign mobs; the TConstruct crossbow/cleaver and arrows are mob gear, not a campaign crafting root. This repository registers no new item/block, recipe JSON, loot table, GUI or acquisition recipe; `src/main/resources/` contains mod metadata and GameTest structures only. Verify third-party mob/mod availability, player access to encounter terrain and progression on the *exact* pack, rather than inventing an item acquisition test for this mod. Administrative `/campaign` status/force/inspect/reset and gated harness spawn commands (`PillagerCampaignsEvents.kt`, `CampaignHarnessCommands.kt`) are diagnostics, not ordinary acquisition.
